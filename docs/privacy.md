# Gridlark privacy policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Gridlark app for Android ("Gridlark", "the app") handles, why, and the choices you have. Gridlark is made by an independent developer ("we", "us").

## The short version

Gridlark works offline. Your puzzle progress and settings stay on your device. There is no account and we do not collect personal data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to show and measure those ads, with your consent where the law requires it. The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play.

## Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

- Your puzzle progress: which puzzles you have opened or solved, the board positions you have saved, times, hints used and the dates you played.
- Your settings: theme, accessibility options, timer, haptics and reminder preferences.
- Simple counters the app uses to space out ads and to give you a free hint each day.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives it. Android may include app data in your device backup if you have backups turned on; that backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings.

## No account, no personal data collected by us

You do not need to sign in or create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information, and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Gridlark shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads are never placed on the puzzle board. To show, limit, measure and protect ads against fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID.
- Your IP address, and an approximate (coarse) location derived from it.
- Information about your device and the app, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version.
- Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped, and diagnostic information about ad performance.

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is used in line with Google's privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).

## Consent

Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Gridlark asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where US state privacy laws apply, the same tool lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising. If consent is required and you do not give it, you may see non-personalised or limited ads instead.

You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices (shown where it applies).

## The "Remove ads" purchase (Google Play Billing)

Gridlark offers an optional one-time purchase to remove ads. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase has been made, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase on your devices. Google's handling of payment information is described in the Google Payments privacy notice: <https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice>

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. Reminders are created on the phone itself; no notification data is sent to us or to a push-notification service. You can turn reminders off in Settings at any time, or block notifications for Gridlark in your Android system settings.

## Children

Gridlark is a general-audience puzzle app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to content rated suitable for teens and older. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app in a way that resulted in data being collected, please contact us and we will help.

## Data retention and deletion

Because your progress and settings are stored only on your device, you control them. Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling Gridlark, permanently deletes all of it. We cannot recover it for you, because we never receive it.

Data collected by Google for advertising is retained and deleted according to Google's policies. You can learn more, and manage your Google ad settings, at <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites> and <https://myadcenter.google.com>

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are best directed to Google for advertising data. You are still welcome to contact us with any question and we will answer it.

## Security

The app stores its data in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Connections made by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Billing are encrypted by Google.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in the app and the effective date above will change. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
