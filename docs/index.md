# Gridlark

Four fresh logic puzzles every day: **Crowns**, **Sun & Moon**, **Trail** and **Sudoku**.

- Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone. No guessing.
- Everyone gets the same puzzles on the same date. Gentle early in the week, trickier at the weekend.
- Works offline. No account, no sign-in. Your progress stays on your phone.
- Never an ad on the puzzle board. One optional, one-time purchase removes ads. No subscriptions.

Gridlark is an Android app made by an independent developer.
